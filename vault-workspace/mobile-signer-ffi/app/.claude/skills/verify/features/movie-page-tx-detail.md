# Movie Page transaction detail (S8)

A tap on a transaction row on Home or on the history screen opens that transaction on board 3.6.2. The row's own figures show at once: รับ or ส่งออก with its arrow, the amount in compact BTC, the status card with six segments toward six confirmations, the date, and the whole TXID with a copy button. When the explorer answers for that txid, a send shows what reached the destination instead of the net with its fee, and the screen adds where the money went, the fee, the rate, the total, the change, and a technical section with every input and output. A send that another party's input helped pay, such as a payjoin or a coinjoin, keeps the net with its fee, names no destination, and shows only the total and the change in its cost card, because what the others got from the wallet and its share of the fee cannot be told apart. An explorer that does not know the txid says so, a failed request gives the reason in Thai, and both offer ลองอีกครั้ง. The demo wallet asks nothing and shows the row only.

## Sub-features

- `mp-s8-open` A tap on a Home row (`home_tx_N`) or a history row (`history_tx_N`) opens S8 (`screen_send/s8`) for that transaction, and `back` or กลับ (`cta_s8_back`) returns to the screen it came from.
- `mp-s8-header` The navy header shows the direction (`tx_direction`), the amount (`tx_amount`), its baht value now once a price arrived (`tx_fiat`) and a TESTNET pill (`tx_testnet`). A send shows the net with รวมค่าธรรมเนียม (`tx_amount_note`) until its detail loads, and then the amount that reached the destination. A send with another party's input keeps the net with รวมค่าธรรมเนียม.
- `mp-s8-status` The status card (`tx_status`) reads ยังไม่ยืนยัน or ยืนยันแล้ว (`tx_status_title`), the count and, once loaded, the block (`tx_confirmations`), six segments up to six confirmations (`tx_progress`) and a hint (`tx_status_hint`). A pending send says ไม่ต้องทำอะไร และอย่าส่งซ้ำ and, once loaded, the rate it pays.
- `mp-s8-where` The date (`tx_date`) of a confirmed transaction, and once loaded the destination of a send the wallet paid alone under ส่งไปที่ or the wallet's own address of a receive under เข้าที่อยู่ของคุณ (`tx_address_N`), grouped by four, each with a copy button (`cta_s8_copy_address_N`).
- `mp-s8-cost` A loaded send shows ค่าธรรมเนียมเครือข่าย in sat and baht (`tx_fee`), the rate (`tx_rate`), รวมออกจากกระเป๋า (`tx_total`) and เงินทอนกลับเข้ากระเป๋า (`tx_change`) in one card (`tx_cost`). The change counts only the wallet's change addresses, not a payment to its own receive address. A send with another party's input shows only the total and the change.
- `mp-s8-txid` The whole TXID in groups of eight (`tx_txid`) and a copy button (`cta_s8_copy_txid`) that copies it without the spaces.
- `mp-s8-technical` รายละเอียดทางเทคนิค (`cta_s8_technical`) opens every input (`tx_input_N`) and output (`tx_output_N`) with whose it is (another party's output reads ปลายทาง only on a send the wallet paid alone), the fee as inputs minus outputs, the rate, the size, the virtual size, the RBF signal and the block (`tx_technical`).
- `mp-s8-states` กำลังโหลดรายละเอียด… (`tx_loading`) while the explorer is asked, ไม่พบธุรกรรมนี้ใน explorer ตอนนี้ (`tx_not_found`) for a txid it does not know, and the Thai reason of a failed request (`tx_failed`) with the raw Rust text under it in a debug build (`debug_failure`). The last two offer ลองอีกครั้ง (`cta_s8_retry`).

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Home → a row under ธุรกรรมล่าสุด, on a live wallet with transactions.
- Home → ดูทั้งหมด → a row of the history screen, on a live wallet with more than four transactions.
- The debug catalog row TRANSACTION · รายละเอียดธุรกรรม ✓ (`catalog_send/s8`). It opens the row tapped last, or a fixed demo send before any tap.

## Gotchas

- S8 asks the explorer each time it opens, for the transaction and then the tip, and never refreshes by itself. Open it again for a newer count.
- A send shows the net with รวมค่าธรรมเนียม until the detail loads and then what reached the destination, so on a live wallet the amount changes once. A payment to the wallet itself keeps the net with รวมค่าธรรมเนียม, because nothing reached anyone else. So does a send with another party's input, such as a payjoin or a coinjoin, because the explorer's answer cannot say what the others got from the wallet or what share of the fee it paid. Its technical section still shows the transaction's own fee and rate.
- The demo wallet never loads a detail. The addresses, the cost card and the technical section need a key or the probe.
- Back while the explorer is asked leaves at once. The request runs on to its end in the background and its answer is dropped, so opening the row again asks again.
- The copy label reads คัดลอกแล้ว for 1.5 s, which is shorter than one `see`, so prove a copy with the paste on Send as above.
- The technical section and กลับ sit below the first screen on a 1080 x 2400 emulator. Swipe up with `verify.sh adb shell input swipe 540 1800 540 600 400` before you tap them.
- Left out for now: the explorer link, share, the note, the time estimate, the sent time of a pending send, and the unknown-outcome banner. No row can open a payment of unknown outcome, because the wallet clears that payment as soon as a loaded history lists its txid.
- The technical section lists every input and output, so a payment with hundreds of outputs draws hundreds of rows.
- The loading line, the not-found line, รวมค่าธรรมเนียม, ไม่มีที่อยู่ and ไม่ส่งสัญญาณ have no Penpot board. Their copy is a default awaiting design review.
