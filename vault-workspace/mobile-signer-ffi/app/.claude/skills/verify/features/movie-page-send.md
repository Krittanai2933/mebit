# Movie Page send (S* / M*)

Single-sig send checks the typed address and amount, shows the fee tiers, reviews the payment and ends on a slip. Without a key the wallet uses an offline demo balance (0.0125 BTC) and a mock broadcast. Multisig vault send collects mock signatures (Jade path is BLE demo without live sign_psbt).

## Sub-features

- `mp-s1-home` shows the single-sig balance.
- `mp-s2-backup-gate` blocks receive until backup confirmed.
- `mp-s3-receive` shows the wallet's address as a QR with copy and share, for a live wallet.
- `mp-s4-send` takes an address, an amount and a fee tier, and says what is wrong with them.
- `mp-s5-review` shows what leaves and what stays, with the baht value when a price arrived, and signs.
- `mp-s6-slip` shows the sent slip with a txid, the send date, the baht value when a price arrived, and a share button.
- `mp-m1-m4` walks vault send → collect → (optional Jade demo) → broadcast mock.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Home first-run → กระเป๋ากุญแจเดียว → ส่ง, or catalog SINGLE-SIG / SEND · VAULT.

## Gotchas

- Fee tiers are 4, 8 and 15 sat/vB times a typical 141 vB payment (564, 1,128 and 2,115 sat), not a live fee estimate.
- Receive leaves out the board's line about a fresh address every time (the hot wallet watches and spends the first address only) and the logo tile the board draws over the QR (it would cover data modules). A wallet that is not live keeps the old screen, with no QR and no share, because money sent to its demo address is lost.
- **Receive, live wallet.** The QR, the address under it and the copy and share buttons side by side show only for a wallet whose snapshot loaded (`live`). That needs a key and a network that reaches mempool.space (the public test phrase through [import seed](./movie-page-import-seed.md)). On 2026-11-02 this Mac could not reach it, so the live screen was checked with a throwaway patch that sets `live = true` and a real testnet address in `SingleSigWallet.useDemoFixture()`, never committed. Open Home first (`id:cta_singlesig`), because Home binds the wallet and the catalog row `id:catalog_send/s3` alone shows an empty card. The row sits below the first screen of the route list: swipe it up three times, `idb ui swipe 201 700 201 300 --duration 0.4 --udid <udid>` on iOS. Then `verify.sh ios-see "id:ss_receive_qr"` and shot `mp-s3-receive`: the QR 196 dp square at the board's x 103, y 208, the address in two lines, copy and share at y 732. To prove the code reads, build the helper once, `swiftc -O .claude/skills/verify/scripts/qrdecode.swift -o /tmp/qrdecode`, then `/tmp/qrdecode <shot>.png` prints the address it found. `verify.sh ios-tap "id:cta_s3_copy"` puts the address on the pasteboard (`xcrun simctl pbpaste <udid>`), and `verify.sh ios-tap "id:cta_s3_share"` opens iOS's sheet with the address as text.
- Home leaves out the bottom tabs, the settings gear and the counterparty names on the transaction rows (a row says รับ or ส่งออก): the app has no tab navigation or settings screen yet, and the wallet knows no counterparty.
- **Home states.** A wallet that is the offline demo shows `0.0125 BTC` with a small demo marker. A wallet with a real key shows the balance with `≈ ฿…` under it and, when its history loaded, ธุรกรรมล่าสุด (`id:home_tx_label`) with up to four rows: รับ or ส่งออก, the date without น., a signed amount (+ green, the real minus U+2212 grey) and the confirmations (0 รอยืนยัน, 1 to 6 ยืนยัน N บล็อก, 7 and more ยืนยันแล้ว). On the first bind of a key (relaunch the app, import the public test phrase, open Home through `id:catalog_send/s1`) the balance reads `—` with กำลังดึงยอด… and no demo marker until the chain answers; if it cannot be reached the dash stays with the retry line and a plain-words error (see Wallet unavailable). The rows need a reachable chain. On 2026-11-02 they were checked through a throwaway patch that gave the demo fixture a Live mode and five history rows (`.audit/phase2-c4b-home-probe.patch`, never committed): 25 of the 27 texts of board 4.1 were within 1.7 dp, and the fifth (unconfirmed) row stayed out because the list stops at four. The page scrolls as a whole when it overflows: expand the key details (`verify.sh ios-tap "id:key_details_toggle"`), then `idb ui swipe 200 750 200 250 --duration 0.4 --udid <udid>` moves everything up by the overflow, the navy block with it, and the status bar keeps a navy strip. The system text size (`xcrun simctl ui <udid> content_size …`) does not change the app's text.
- The send form leaves out the QR scan button: the app has no camera path yet. The ฿ toggle, the gold baht line, the baht fee prices and `ใช้ได้ ฿…` need a price: without one the form is BTC and sats only, as before.
- Review and the slip ask for the price once per screen and wait four seconds at most. On a dead network a screen stays without baht until it is opened again, and the fee reads in sats.
- After a mock payment Home reads 0.0125 BTC again, because a wallet without a key resets its demo balance whenever Home opens. Read the payment on Review and the slip instead.
- A share that fails does nothing and shows nothing: the board has no error for it.
- The banner under the review card follows only the sign attempt made on that screen: `id:sign_refused` for a refused Face ID and `id:error` for a failed broadcast. A refusal wins when both happened, and a wallet error left over from Home does not show there. With the banner up, the inline `ยอดไม่พอ` line of the unconnected demo wallet sits below the visible area.
- On the 852 dp tall iPhone 15 the banner is cut about 12 dp at the bottom by the sign button, because the board is drawn for 874 dp. The content scrolls, so scrolling shows it whole.
- The sign button reads เซ็นด้วย Face ID with the app lock on, เซ็นและส่ง for a live wallet, and เซ็นบนเครื่องนี้ (demo) otherwise.
- S* chain/PSBT paths are mocked unless the wallet is live; M* is mocked.
- M3 Jade is simulator-safe demo only.
