# Movie Page send (S* / M*)

Single-sig send checks the typed address and amount against the spendable balance, shows the host's live fee tiers, reviews the payment and ends on a slip. Review shows the fee, amount and total the core quotes for the real destination and coins, and a signed payment that differs from that quote stops on Review for one more confirm. Without a key the wallet uses an offline demo balance (0.0125 BTC) and a mock payment. Multisig vault send collects signatures from the vault's own keys, soft-signed on this phone or signed live on a Jade over Bluetooth, and its broadcast is still a mock.

## Sub-features

- `mp-s1-home` shows the single-sig balance and the last four transactions, every amount in compact BTC.
- `mp-s1-pull` reloads Home's balance and transactions when the page is pulled down.
- `mp-s2-backup-gate` blocks receive until backup confirmed.
- `mp-s3-receive` shows the wallet's address as a QR with copy and share, for a live wallet.
- `mp-s4-send` takes an address, an amount and a fee tier, checks the amount against the spendable balance, and says what is wrong with them.
- `mp-s4-keypad` closes the keyboard with the เสร็จ chip or a tap outside the address and amount fields.
- `mp-s4-fees` shows the host's live fee rates, or the board's fixed rates with a grey line when no host answers.
- `mp-s4-scan` opens the camera over Send to read a payment QR into the address field, and asks for the camera the first time.
- `mp-s5-review` shows what leaves and what stays from the core's quote of this payment (`hot_quote_send`), with the baht value when a price arrived, and signs exactly what it quoted. Sign stays grey while the quote loads, and a quote that fails shows why with ลองอีกครั้ง and no fee.
- `mp-s5-high-fee` asks once more before posting a signed payment whose fee, amount or folded change is not the quote's, and for a rate above 2,000 sat/vB, a fixed amount whose fee is above it, or change folded into the fee.
- `mp-s5-pin` asks for the session Spending PIN before signing on a phone with no screen lock, which sets it before Home.
- `mp-s5-repost` offers ส่งรายการเดิมอีกครั้ง on Review and Send after a payment of unknown outcome. It posts the same signed bytes behind the signing owner check, keeps the guard unless the answer is that the payment is out, and was driven on the Android emulator in run `20261106-012722`.
- `mp-s5-replace` offers ยกเลิกรายการนี้ at once and เพิ่มค่าธรรมเนียมแล้วส่งใหม่ after a refused retry, on Review and Send, for a payment of unknown outcome. Either signs behind the owner check, shows its card, and posts on its confirm. A cancel that is out keeps PAYMENT UNCONFIRMED until a block holds one transaction of its chain. Review offers none of the guard's controls while its own post runs. Driven on the Android emulator with a probe in runs `20261106-132510` and `20261106-184650`.
- `mp-s6-slip` shows the sent slip with the amount and fee that actually left, a txid, the send date, the baht value when a price arrived, and a share button.
- `mp-m1-m4` walks vault send → collect → (optional live Jade sign) → broadcast mock.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Home first-run → กระเป๋ากุญแจเดียว → ส่ง, or catalog SINGLE-SIG / SEND · VAULT.

## Gotchas

- Fee tiers come from the testnet4 host in use when Send opens, as sat/vB times a typical 141 vB payment. The app tries the build's own node first, when `vault-workspace/mobile-signer-ffi/esplora-host.local` named one at `ffi.sh build` time, then blockstream.info, then mempool.space. On testnet4 all three tiers have read 1 sat/vB (open item 72). The tier names and times are the board's. Before the first answer of an app run arrives, and whenever no host answers, the cards show the board's fixed 4, 8 and 15 sat/vB (564, 1,128 and 2,115 sat). Only the no-answer case adds the small grey line `id:fee_fallback` under them, which reads ใช้ค่าธรรมเนียมโดยประมาณ เพราะดึงค่าจริงไม่ได้. The tiles and Send's balance check stay estimates at the type's typical size. Review shows the fee the core quotes for this payment's coins, `… sat` with no `≈`, and the slip shows the fee the signed transaction pays, which is that fee unless the coins changed before the sign, when Review asked first. An emulator reaches the own node through the Mac's Tailscale route when Tailscale is up. `verify.sh adb shell svc wifi disable` and `svc data disable` before opening Send show the fallback. Rust remembers a live answer for five minutes and a fallback for thirty seconds.
